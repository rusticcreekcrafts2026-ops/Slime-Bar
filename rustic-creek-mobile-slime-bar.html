How it works

Three steps from empty table to a jar of slime each guest made themselves.

1

We set up

We arrive with everything needed for the party. There is nothing for you to buy or bring.

2

Guests build

Everyone chooses a base, color, scent and add-ins, then mixes their own batch with guidance.

3

Take it home

Each guest leaves with a jar of slime they made themselves.
